- NOVA FPV explosive drones: finite charges, battery, signal range, collision, destructibility and server-owned detonation.
- NOVA Research laboratory landmark with five FPV stands per sector, ordinary loot chests, RAM SHIELD, TURBO DRIVE, BOMB RACK and AEGIS suit equipment.

- AEGIS is an original combined flight suit: finite energy flight, pulse shots, charged beam and a purpose-built HUD.
- All new persistent structures and laboratory pickups participate in the network delta system.
