# PRIDA 0.38 — Mobility / Rotorcraft

Stage B of the four-part gameplay expansion.

- Civilian cars preserve lateral momentum, support handbrake drifting and can leave dedicated ramps with ballistic motion and hard-landing damage.
- SKYHAWK helicopter: player-controlled rotorcraft with climb/descent, strafe, finite machine-gun ammunition and six TALON rockets. Air-defence systems treat hostile airborne helicopters as valid targets.
- KITE wings: unpowered gliding gear. Diving trades height for speed; pulling up trades speed for height. It cannot hover or create energy.
- Big City gains localized smooth hills, ridges and basins while roads, entrances, airfields and tile seams remain at grade.
- Existing 0.37 activity streaming, finite HYDRA ammunition, tank armour, bus camera and lift-network repair are retained.

Controls

- Car drift: hold Space while steering at speed.
- Helicopter: E enter/exit, W/S forward/reverse, A/D strafe, Space climb, C descend, mouse heading, RMB machine gun, Q rockets.
- KITE: equip KITE WINGS and hold Space while airborne. Aim down for speed, pull up to exchange speed for height.

This release keeps the current procedural helicopter and wing models so no new third-party asset license is required.
