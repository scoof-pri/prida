What's new · PRIDA 0.28

Duels in the Big City. New quick match DUEL · BIG CITY (and a group mode): a 1 v 1 to five eliminations inside a 300 m ring in the middle of the city; outside the ring hurts. Online play at up to 100 ms ping: your own walking shows at once and the server only corrects it gently; other players move smoothly between states instead of jumping; shots are checked where you saw the target (the server rewinds by your ping plus that delay). Duels run at 60 steps and 30 states a second, and states are about half the size: chests and destruction only go out when they change, and the group only when it changes. Everything on a roof is solid now: parapets, air-conditioning units, water tanks, skylights, antennas, vents and chimneys stop you and your shots, and go when the roof does. The third-person camera no longer passes through walls: it starts from a point that is never inside a wall, keeps its whole view clear of it and comes all the way in when you stand against one.

PRIDA 0.27.1

The toon soldiers are back. Players and bots are the three cartoon soldiers again — Ranger, Hazmat and Outrider, in every skin and bot colour — with their own animations and the guns in their hands. Everything from 0.27 stays: items, bare hands (now a proper boxing guard), four bosses, the tutorial. The legs still follow the way you move while the upper body aims.

PRIDA 0.27

Everyone drops in with bare hands. No starting weapons, gear or medkits any more: punch, loot, survive. Chests now hold a weapon and an item, and there are twelve new ones: SHIELD CELL and SHIELD KEG charge your armour, BANDAGES, OVERCHARGE and the REGEN SERUM heal, RUSH makes you faster and STIM gives endless stamina; the IMPULSE grenade throws people through the air and the CLINGER sticks to whatever it hits, a player included; the LAUNCH PAD throws you up and the glider brings you down, the COVER WALL stops bullets and the CAMPFIRE heals everyone beside it. Hold an item and press fire to use it. Chests come in tiers (door, park, supply, roof and legendary, with light beams on the roofs), restock in Training, and the fallen drop everything they carried. Four bosses are left — IGNIS, NULL, ARACHNE and TERRA — and a relic takes its own place in your belt. Animation: characters strafe and backpedal with their legs while the chest keeps aiming, turn on the spot with a step, raise their fists in a guard, drink, open chests and take off into jumps. Effects everywhere: chests bursting in their colour, item auras, armour breaking, eliminations, footstep dust, campfire flames. The battle bus has seats, lights and clouds around it, and you hear its engine and the wind. New: a two-minute TUTORIAL (below, and in SOLO MODE).

PRIDA 0.26

A battle-royale HUD. Health and armour are two slanted bars bottom-left with their numbers; your five weapon slots sit bottom-right in their rarity colours, the one in your hands raised, with its ammunition above; the map is top-right with the players left, the clock and the zone under it; eliminations run down the left side with the weapon that did it. Hits you land now show damage numbers over the target — blue when armour took part of it, gold for a heavy or finishing hit — on players and bosses alike. Walls no longer burst into white cubes: the pieces are broken lumps of concrete with real grain, tinted like the wall they came from.

PRIDA 0.25

A new lobby. Your operator now stands on a lit platform high over the edge of the map, with the city behind, wearing everything you have equipped and holding your primary. Drag the character to turn it. Point at anything in COSMETICS to try it on — skins, headgear, weapon colours, patterns, charms, effects — and emotes play on the stage. Cards come in rarity colours (common, uncommon, rare, epic, legendary); the first tap on an item you do not own picks it, the second buys it, so nothing is bought by accident. In INVENTORY, the weapon or gear under the pointer is in your hands. In a private group your friends stand beside you on the stage. Weapon icons are re-rendered with the new finishes.

PRIDA 0.24

Real surfaces on everything that was still flat. Weapons are finished like the real thing: powder-coated steel, stippled polymer, oiled walnut, rubber, polished blades and brass, and a weapon colour is now a coating on the steel. Your first-person gloves and sleeves are woven cloth, magazines are steel with brass rounds. Cars have dark reflective glass, glossy paint, chrome, rubber tyres and road dirt towards the ground; street barriers are concrete, dumpsters worn metal, poles wood. The loot chest, the battle bus and its balloon, furniture metal, rooftop plant, window frames and the bosses' armour get scratched steel instead of tread plate. Every moving thing keeps its own grain as it moves.

PRIDA 0.23

Accounts and friends. Press LOG IN in the lobby to create an account with a name and a password. Your coins, wardrobe, battle pass, skills and loadout are saved to it and follow you to any device. Every account has a friend code: add friends by code, see who is online, and invite them from your group with one tap; they get a JOIN banner wherever they are. Accounts live on the PRIDA server; without one, progress stays on your device as before.

PRIDA 0.22

New combat operators. Players and bots are soldiers with real proportions now, not toys: combat uniforms with camouflage, gloves and boots, a helmet with goggles, a plate carrier with magazine pouches, a radio and a pack, a belt, a thigh holster, knee and elbow pads. Three kits — RANGER (woodland), HAZMAT (rubber suit, hood and gas mask) and OUTRIDER (urban camo, cap and headset) — and bots come in desert, urban, navy, woodland, olive, winter, black and coyote. Body armour shows as side plates, pauldrons and a throat guard. Every weapon is held properly with both hands: rifles in the shoulder, pistols at arm's length, the launcher on the shoulder, the minigun at the hip, blades and grenades in the hand; the gun follows your aim up and down and rolls over for a reload. New animation set: walk, jog and sprint at the right speed, jumps, landings, hits, a kneeling medkit, swings and overhand throws, and new emotes. Your first-person sleeves and gloves match your kit.

PRIDA 0.21.2

Third-person shots land on the crosshair. The camera sits behind your shoulder, so shots used to fly parallel to the crosshair and hit to one side of it, more the farther away the target. Now the game finds what the crosshair is on — a wall, a player, a boss — and fires from your character straight at it. The camera moves to the right shoulder, stops orbiting under you when you look up, and your own name and health bar no longer float over the crosshair. Tracers leave the gun instead of the head. Fixed: standing still, flying or paused, characters' upper bodies tumbled head over heels (the aim bend was added again every frame); arm poses for free fall, the glider bar and reloads now show.

PRIDA 0.21.1

When the browser will not give the game 3D graphics, the loading screen now says why and what to do, and PRIDA asks again a few times and with lighter settings before it gives up. After the graphics driver crashes during a match, the next start uses safer settings (Fast, 75 % resolution, low textures) so it does not crash again, and the pause screen offers a reload when the browser does not bring 3D back.

PRIDA 0.21

Eight bosses, and every one fights with a move set. Three new bosses: GELU the Frost Wraith on the lookout hill (ice shards that freeze you for a moment, a frost nova, a line of ice spikes; drops the FROST HEART: F freezes everyone around you), ARACHNE the Brood Mother in the pine grove (webs that slow you, a pounce onto you, a pool of venom; drops the VENOM FANG: F lays a venom pool where you aim, G pounces), and TERRA the Quarry Wyrm in the quarry pit (it spits rock, sweeps its tail, and burrows to burst out right under you; drops the EARTH SPINE: F sends a line of stone spikes out of the ground). The five old bosses were rebuilt in more detail and learned new moves: VIS charges through trees and throws boulders torn from its back, IGNIS calls down meteors, NOEMA bursts out a ring of psychic bolts, ENTROPIA brings down a storm of shards, NULL opens a gravity well that drags you in before it collapses. Every move has its own wind-up animation, and the ground where it will land is marked and fills up until it hits: step out of the glow. Bosses below 40 % health rage. Players no longer spawn next to a living boss.

Animations and effects: reloads with a real magazine that drops out of the weapon while the other hand brings up a fresh one and charges it; shotguns fed shell by shell, revolvers that dump their cases and take a speed-loader, launchers loaded from behind, grenades drawn from the belt. Brass cases (red shells from shotguns) fly out of the gun, bounce and lie on the ground; a star-shaped muzzle flash lights up the gun, your hands and the wall beside you. The view bobs and rolls with each step, the weapon sways as you turn and is carried low while sprinting, the view widens when you sprint and in free fall. Characters lean into a sprint and the jetpack, dive spread-eagled out of the bus, hang from the glider with both hands on the bar, tip the gun down to reload and crouch to heal; the bus opens its rear hatch when someone jumps, and its propellers turn.

Fixed: characters never turned to face where they aim (they all looked one way since 0.18); holding the right mouse button to aim and then firing did nothing, and letting go of the aim while firing left it stuck; one oversized message could crash the online server with every room on it; on a lagging connection DASH could repeat itself and the C4 detonator could set off charges placed later; a C4 that hit a boss hung in the air; the Chaos Herald's line of ruin hurt the herald itself; a boss's quake hurt players twice; textures were served with the wrong type and nothing was cached; characters, charms and relics leaked graphics memory when they were removed.

PRIDA 0.20.4

Third person is now the default view (T switches). Your character turns with the camera at once, and every character bends at the waist to aim up and down. Fixed: loot chests were not drawn at all since 0.20 (you could open an invisible chest); furniture, cars and other props built from open shells no longer turn see-through from some angles; a malformed connection request can no longer crash the online server.

PRIDA 0.20.3

A ballistic shield, three grenades, body armour and a third-person camera. The shield is gear like the jetpack: carried on your left arm, it stops most of what hits you from the front until it gives way, and slows you down while you hold it. Three grenades are thrown on a fuse and go off wherever they have rolled to: FRAG tears the place apart, FLASH blinds everyone who can see it (bots included) for a few seconds, SMOKE lays down a cloud nobody — and no bot — can see through. Body armour now comes as vests: a light vest (+50) and a plate carrier (+100, a little slower), both visible on the character and left behind when they fall. Press T (or choose the camera in Graphics & map) for the third-person view: the camera swings behind your shoulder, pulls in when a wall is in the way, and you can see your own gear, vest and shield.

PRIDA 0.20.2

New bosses. None of them is a robot any more: VIS is a colossus of pale granite with boulders on its shoulders and gold fists, IGNIS a stooped golem of black basalt with lava in its cracks, NOEMA a hovering robe with a blank mask and orbs of thought, NULL an armoured warden with a heavy cloak and a ring of nothing at its back, ENTROPIA a core of light inside a cage of turning shards. The bodies are built by the game, not imported, and every move — standing, walking, striking, casting, falling — is animated in code, which is what the next stage builds on.

PRIDA 0.20.1

The C4 remote charge: throw it with the fire button and it sticks where it lands (up to four at once), then the aim button (RMB, or ⊕ on touch) sets every charge off. It is a demolition charge, so it takes walls and floors apart — and it does not care who threw it, so keep your distance. Found in chests like the other power weapons. New solo mode Debug · sandbox: no storm, no timer, no rewards, and the J key (or the DEBUG MENU button) opens a menu that hands you any weapon at any rarity, gear, medkits, armor and ammunition, adds or clears target bots, and toggles invulnerability, flight and infinite ammunition.

PRIDA 0.20

Stage 1 of 4 of the big remake: a new look for the whole world, and faster. Every surface has real textures with normal and roughness maps and reflects the sky: brick, plaster and concrete-panel facades with cornices, plinths, sills and lintels; clay-tile and slate roofs with chimneys, flat roofs with parapets and rooftop plant, factory sheds and smokestacks; asphalt, paving, kerbs and gravel. Windows have frames and reflective glass, cars are glossy, and flats inside have painted walls. Real 3D grass sways in the wind and bends around players; new broadleaf and pine trees, bushes, ferns, flowers and reeds, rocks, cacti and hay bales. A sky with drifting clouds lights the scene, and a collapsed building leaves heaps of broken masonry. The game now draws only what you can see: nothing hidden behind walls and buildings, no furniture in flats you cannot look into, no back faces. A busy street draws about a tenth of the triangles it used to. Coming next: new models and a new lobby (stage 2), animations and effects (stage 3), and new gameplay with interiors (stage 4).

PRIDA 0.19

Character levels and a skill tree. Coins from matches and eliminations buy ranks on two sides: STRENGTH (melee damage, reload speed, firepower, damage taken) and MOBILITY (movement speed, sprint drain, sprint recharge, jump height). Every rank is a level. Ten ranks on the STRENGTH side unlock CYBER STRIKE (30 % chance a melee hit kills outright) and MOMENTUM (30 % chance a reload finishes instantly); ten on MOBILITY unlock DASH (V, or the DASH button) and a sprint that recharges 50 % faster. Bonuses are small, capped, and the server clamps them.

PRIDA 0.18

Wardrobe and battle pass. Weapon colours, patterns and charms, player skins, head accessories, personal effects and emotes (B, or the EMOTE button) — squat dance, spin dance, robot, jump jacks, wave, nod, head shake. The battle pass has thirty free tiers: matches, eliminations and wins earn XP, and each tier hands over a cosmetic straight away.

What's new · PRIDA 0.17

New bosses and their relics. VIS the Strength Colossus (forest) drops the TITAN GLOVES: every melee hit is a one-shot kill, and F shakes the ground. IGNIS the Fire Golem (desert) drops the EMBER CORE: a ring of fire and two helper bots. NOEMA the Mind Oracle (glade) drops the MIND CROWN: the enemy you aim at sees only a flashback of their own past for 5 s. ENTROPIA the Chaos Herald (meadow) drops the CHAOS SHARD: once a match, a cataclysm tears half the map apart, and F opens a line of ruin ahead of you. NULL the Void Warden still guards the lake with the portal gun. Smarter bots: they regroup on a squadmate in a fight, break contact and heal when badly hurt, lean out of cover to shoot, keep firing at where you ducked, and run out of a ring of fire. New effects: quake shockwaves, the rift seam, the cataclysm flash, gold sparks on a one-shot kill, and camera shake to match.

What's new · PRIDA 0.16

The Big City map is now about 10× the district: a city core ringed by wilderness, with forest to the west, desert to the east, and glades, meadows and lakes to the north and south. Upper storeys hold real flats, each with a kitchen, sofa and TV, bedroom and bathroom, plus pictures and plants on every floor. Windows are real openings with clear glass: you can see in and out, one hit shatters a pane, and then you can climb through.

PRIDA 0.15

Direct play: in a private group, the host's computer runs the match and friends connect straight to it (WebRTC), so the server no longer adds lag. Quick match still runs on the server.

PRIDA 0.14

Four bosses guard the wild: IGNIS the Fire Golem (desert), NOEMA the Mind Oracle (glade), NULL the Void Warden (lake island) and HIEMS the Frost Titan (forest). Each drops a relic: FIRE GLOVES (F: ring of fire, G: two helper bots), MIND CROWN (F: the enemy you aim at sees only a flashback of their own past view for 5 s), VOID PORTAL GUN (F / G: blue and orange portals you can walk and fall through, with a live view through them) and FROST HEART (F: frost nova that freezes enemies). New biomes: forest, lake, glade, meadow and desert. Royales start in the Battle Bus: jump out when you like, free-fall and glide down. Minimap, view-distance setting, distance culling and automatic resolution for smoother frames.

PRIDA 0.13

Online: Quick Match for Big City Royale (up to 10 players, the rest are bots marked BOT) and 1v1 Duels to 5 eliminations, plus private groups with a mode choice. New Big City map, about 8× the old district, with 24 contenders in solo. Brick walls no longer show square patches at low texture quality, ceilings are lit, and far-away furniture is no longer drawn.

PRIDA 0.12

The Cinematic look is now the default. PICTURE also has a render resolution setting (100–50 %) and texture quality (512 px down to 64 px; Medium · 256 px by default).

PRIDA 0.11

No hidden building hit points: a building only falls when its walls really fail, so shooting or rocketing the roof can blow the roof off but never brings the house down. Picture settings (PICTURE in the lobby and the pause menu): a colour-grading shader with exposure, brightness, contrast, saturation, vibrance, gamma, warmth, tint, vignette, sharpness, film grain and bloom, plus presets. New sandbox code 112358: a legendary COMET rocket launcher (solo only, rewards off).

PRIDA 0.10

Everything breaks. Floors and ceilings are concrete blocks too: blast a hole in a ceiling and the furniture above drops through. Roofs, trees, rocks, crates, park benches, stairs and burnt-out cars can all be destroyed. Every building now has a purpose and is furnished on every floor: homes with kitchens, dining and living rooms, bedrooms and bathrooms; cafes, pharmacies, bookshops, markets, hotels, offices, banks, clinics and hospital wards, a university, a station, a mall, warehouses and workshops. Block stairs lead to every floor. Chairs, lamps and small things stop bullets and break.

PRIDA 0.9.1

Every storey is destructible now, not just the ground floor. Upper floors are built from the same concrete blocks, with windows and floor slabs: shoot holes in them, blast them open, and knock out enough of one storey's walls to bring it and everything above it down while the floors below keep standing. Only the roofs are still solid models.

PRIDA 0.9

Block-by-block destruction. Walls are built from concrete blocks: bullets knock them out and drill holes you can shoot and see through, rockets and grenades blast whole sections apart, and knives, the shovel and the katana cut slots into the concrete. Blocks left hanging fall out, a wall cut through no longer carries the floors above, and enough cuts or rockets bring the whole building down in a shower of blocks. Windows shatter, and wrecked walls and facades throw real debris.

PRIDA 0.8.2

Tabbed lobby menu. Buildings, cars and street props no longer appear plain white on strict hosts. Large buildings keep their real proportions: long blocks are built from several wings, and upper storeys share the colour of the ground floor.

PRIDA 0.8

Destructible district: rockets and grenades blow holes in walls, cars explode and burn, and a building that loses its ground floor collapses. Furnished interiors, windows with curtains, doors, street lamps, traffic lights, parked cars and dumpsters. Hills, a lookout and a quarry pit. Textured streets, walls and floors, haze and chimney smoke. Smarter bots that move in squads, share sightings, take cover and loot, with randomised uniforms. New reload, weapon-switch, landing, spawn and death animations.
